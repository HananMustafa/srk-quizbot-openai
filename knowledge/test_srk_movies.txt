Shah Rukh Khan Test Movie Knowledge

Dilwale Dulhania Le Jayenge

Released: 1995
Director: Aditya Chopra
Producer: Yash Chopra
Shah Rukh Khan character: Raj Malhotra

The story follows Raj and Simran, who meet during a trip through Europe. Raj falls in love with Simran and later travels to India to win over her family.

Important visual and scene clues include Switzerland, European countryside, trains, Raj wearing a leather jacket, Simran's traditional clothing, and the mustard field sequence.

Devdas

Released: 2002
Director: Sanjay Leela Bhansali
Producer: Bharat Shah
Shah Rukh Khan character: Devdas Mukherjee

The film is a tragic romantic drama about Devdas, his love for Paro, and his destructive relationship with alcohol.

Chak De India

Released: 2007
Director: Shimit Amin
Producer: Aditya Chopra
Shah Rukh Khan character: Kabir Khan

Kabir Khan coaches the Indian women's national hockey team after facing controversy during his playing career.

Swades

Released: 2004
Director: Ashutosh Gowariker
Producer: Ashutosh Gowariker
Shah Rukh Khan character: Mohan Bhargava

Mohan Bhargava is an Indian NASA project manager who returns to India and becomes involved in improving the life of a rural village.
